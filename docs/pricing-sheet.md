## Quote Guidelines

Use these rules when responding to a client inquiry:

1. **Never quote under $75.** Below that, my time investment exceeds the value.
2. **Anchor on the tier first**, then discuss add-ons. Prevents undercharging.
3. **Always separate service fee from third-party costs.** Client must know Retell minutes are billed to them.
4. **50% deposit is non-negotiable** until I have 5+ reviews.
5. **Discount room:** Up to 15% for repeat clients or bundled maintenance contracts.
6. **Rush jobs:** Add 25% if delivery is requested under 3 days.

## When to Raise Prices

- After every 5 completed jobs with positive reviews: raise by 20-30%
- After first 10 jobs: move to Premium tiers for new clients only
- Never lower prices retroactively for existing clients
- Always honor a quote once given, even if scope grew slightly

---

## When to Decline Work

Say no to:

- Projects under $75 budget
- Clients who want "just a quick fix" for free
- Clients who won't provide their own API keys (billing risk falls on me)
- Projects outside my current capability (I refer them elsewhere)
- Timelines under 3 days (quality risk)
