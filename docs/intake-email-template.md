# Intake Email Template

Use this when a potential client first reaches out. Replace bracketed placeholders with specifics.

---

## Subject Line

```
Re: [Client's Original Subject] — Multi-Agent Automation Project
```

Or if starting a new thread:

```
Your Multi-Agent Automation Project — Next Steps
```

---

## Body

Hi [CLIENT NAME],

Thanks for reaching out about [BRIEF RESTATEMENT OF THEIR NEED].

Before I can give you an accurate quote, I need to understand your project better. Could you answer these four questions:

**1. What exactly do you want automated?**
Not "AI agents" in general — but the specific task. For example: "create a voice bot that handles customer FAQs," or "auto-generate a landing page and deploy it whenever we push code."

**2. What services do you already pay for?**
Retell AI? A voice provider? A CRM? GitHub? Netlify? This tells me what I can connect to versus what we'd need to set up.

**3. Who owns the accounts?**
Best practice is that you own all accounts (Retell, GitHub, Netlify, etc.) and provide me with API keys. This keeps billing, data, and control in your hands. Is that workable for you?

**4. What's your timeline and budget range?**
Just a rough range is fine. It helps me match the deliverable to what you need and what fits your budget.

---

## What Happens Next

Once I have those answers, I'll send you:

- A **scope-of-work document** describing exactly what will be built
- A **fixed price** and **timeline** (usually 3-5 business days from deposit)
- **Payment terms** (50% deposit, 50% on delivery)

You'll have a chance to review and ask questions before any payment.

## Notes for Me (Not For Client)

- Keep the first reply within 24 hours of their initial message
- Never send pricing in the first reply — always ask the four questions first
- If they push for an immediate number, give a range: "$150 to $400 depending on scope"
- If they answer all four questions clearly, proceed to the scope-of-work document
- If they dodge a question (especially about account ownership), that's a red flag
- Save any quotes given in this conversation for future reference
